For the discussion

  1. Shuffling the frame order costs 3.80 dB. Both architectural priors together are worth 2.86 dB. So is the contribution the ray-based architecture, or the video data?
  2. Every target view is drawn from the same index window as the inputs, so it always sits between them, at train and at test time. Is there any experiment in this setting that separates recovered geometry from learned interpolation?
  3. RayZer beats the oracles on DL3DV and RealEstate, where the poses come from COLMAP, and loses on Objaverse, where they are exact. The paper conjectures COLMAP caps the baselines. Is it that, or a protocol that favours a model picking its own camera?
